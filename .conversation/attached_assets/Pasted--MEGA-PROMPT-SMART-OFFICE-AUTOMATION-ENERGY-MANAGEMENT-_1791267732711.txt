# MEGA PROMPT: SMART OFFICE AUTOMATION & ENERGY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## 1. Project Title

**Smart Office Automation & Energy Management System**

Build a complete, modern, intelligent office-management platform that automatically manages:

- 💡 Office lighting
- 🌡️ Temperature and HVAC
- 🏢 Meeting rooms
- 👥 Occupancy detection
- 🖥️ Office equipment
- ⚡ Energy consumption
- 📅 Employee schedules
- 🔐 User requirements and permissions
- 📊 Energy analytics
- 🚨 Alerts and notifications

The system should reduce energy wastage, improve employee comfort, optimize meeting-room usage, and provide administrators with real-time control and analytics.

---

# 2. Main Objective

Create an intelligent system that continuously monitors office occupancy, schedules, room conditions, equipment usage, and energy consumption.

The system should automatically make decisions such as:

- Turn lights ON when people enter a room.
- Turn lights OFF when a room becomes vacant.
- Automatically adjust brightness according to occupancy and daylight.
- Maintain comfortable temperature based on occupancy and user preferences.
- Reduce HVAC operation when rooms are empty.
- Automatically prepare meeting rooms before scheduled meetings.
- Turn off unnecessary equipment after office hours.
- Detect unused rooms and release/cancel their reservation after a configurable grace period.
- Monitor energy consumption in real time.
- Detect unusual energy consumption.
- Recommend energy-saving actions.
- Allow authorized users to manually override automation.
- Return to automatic mode after the override expires.
- Provide administrators with complete monitoring and control.

# 5. Occupancy Management

Implement occupancy detection.

Sensors can provide:

- Motion
- PIR detection
- People count
- Door entry/exit
- Bluetooth/Wi-Fi presence
- Camera-based occupancy count (optional)

Create an occupancy engine.

Example:

IF occupancy > 0:

    room.status = OCCUPIED

ELSE:

    room.status = VACANT

Use configurable vacancy timeout.

Example:

    vacancy_timeout = 10 minutes

If no movement is detected for 10 minutes:

    room.status = VACANT

Do not immediately turn devices off because temporary inactivity may occur.
